Meet Simon

Simon Flannery is a BSc Sports Science graduate who has been in the health and wellness industry for the last 28 years. Since completing his degree in 1995, he's built a career around one simple belief: understanding what's really going on in your body — especially the impact of stress — is the key to lasting health.

After university, Simon travelled extensively through South East Asia, where he learned Reflexology in Penang, Malaysia — specifically a Taiwanese technique called Rho Shur, a much deeper treatment compared to conventional approaches. On returning to the UK, he gained formal qualifications in both reflexology and massage to give his clients a truly holistic approach to health and fitness.

Simon was club manager for celebrity trainer Matt Roberts, based in 5-star hotels in Kensington and Mayfair. He went solo in 2004, operating as a freelance personal trainer from the Metropolitan Hotel in Mayfair where he remains to this day — spending the rest of his week at home in Southsea, Portsmouth.

Services

From one-to-one training to deep-tissue healing — every service is built around understanding your body and what it needs.

Personal Training

A 90-minute initial assessment — online or in person — covering mobility, stabilisation, functional movement, and strength. Includes exercise prescription and nutritional guidance. Follow-up sessions are 1 hour.

90 min assessment 1 hr sessions

Group Training

High-energy 60-minute sessions for 2–10 participants. Great for building camaraderie, accountability, and results — whether you're training with friends, colleagues, or a local community group.

60 min 2–10 people

Reflexology

60 or 90-minute treatments using Rho Shur — a deeper Taiwanese technique learned in Malaysia. Simon works reflex points on the feet to ease tension and encourage deep relaxation. Most clients find it so restorative they book regular sessions as part of their overall wellbeing.

60 min 90 min Rho Shur

Massage

60 or 90-minute therapeutic treatments covering deep tissue and sports massage, with techniques that support recovery alongside any medical care you're receiving. Whether you're managing chronic tension or training hard, Simon tailors pressure and technique to your needs.

60 min 90 min Deep Tissue
